About the photograph

M31 - Andromeda Galaxy

The Andromeda Galaxy (M31), located approximately 2.5 million light-years from Earth, is our nearest major galactic neighbor. This image reveals its luminous core, intricate spiral arms, and dark dust lanes, alongside its companion galaxies M32 and M110.
