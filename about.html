About

An amateur astrophotographer, learning with every clear night.

Learning under
the Auburn sky.

I’ve always been interested in space and astronomy, which is part of what eventually led me toward engineering. I studied mechanical engineering at Purdue University Fort Wayne and I'm currently working toward my Ph.D. at Auburn University, with my research focused on thermal-fluid science and additive manufacturing.

I got started with astrophotography on May 26, 2021, after watching a YouTube video by Johnny Harris about photographing a galaxy. I figured I would give it a try myself, and it quickly became one of my favorite hobbies.

I spent a lot of my first nights at the Auburn Red Barn with my roommate Benny, figuring out how to use a camera, find targets, and get usable images. Since then, I’ve slowly upgraded my equipment and learned a lot more about imaging and processing.

I’ve kept some of those early photos on this site along with my newer work. They’re not perfect, but I like being able to look back and see how much I’ve learned since I started.
